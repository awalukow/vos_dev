# Abstract ticketing emblem

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Replaces literal VOS letterforms with flowing choral ribbons, a subtle V-shaped convergence and an S-like central curve. Retains the plum, mauve and pink palette.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a completely redesigned abstract emblem for Voice of Soul Choir's concert ticketing website. This is a substantial departure from the prior logo, NOT a rearrangement of it.
Reference 1: ASJ logo, inspiration only for dignified architectural vertical rhythm and concert institution elegance; do not reproduce its organ pipes or its outline.
Reference 2: original VOS logo, use ONLY its plum/mauve/pink color family, NOT its shapes or musical notes or readable VOS.
Primary design: a single integrated sculptural symbol of rising choral voices, like three elegant interlocking upright ribbons forming an open fan or resonant acoustic arch. Sophisticated architectural silhouette, beautifully balanced negative space. A subtle V may emerge from the inner negative space and an S-like flow from a ribbon, but NO readable letters, NO literal V O S, no circle-and-triangle alphabet, NO musical note icons. Emphasize a unique cohesive flowing emblem rather than separate objects. Contemporary performing-arts identity with graceful geometric curves and restrained vertical energy.
Palette: solid plum #6a3d7b, mauve #b365a5, soft pink #dfabd1. Plum is dominant, pink is a restrained accent. Flat solid fills, crisp smooth edges, no gradients, texture, shadows or fine fragile lines.
One emblem only, no wording, no labels, no presentation board. Square composition with symbol occupying 85% of canvas, centered, actual transparent background. Must be recognizable at 64px on a pale website header.
