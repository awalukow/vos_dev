# Ticketing logo

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The new ticketing mark retains the existing VOS geometric letterforms and plum, mauve, and pink palette. Ascending musical stems introduce the vertical rhythm of the ASJ reference; a curved stage line connects the composition. The website pairs the mark with live, accessible stacked lettering.

Simplify and unify into a polished contemporary cultural institution identity. Bold enough for a 64px website header; ample clean negative space, deliberate balanced geometry, flat vector-like shapes, crisp edges.
Palette restricted to existing VOS plum #6a3d7b, mauve #b365a5, and soft pink #dfabd1. No red or black. Transparent background, no white panel. Only the emblem, no accompanying words or captions. One symbol centered and filling most of a square canvas with narrow safe margins. No mockup, no texture, no shadows, no gradients, no decorative extras.
